The frog coin: Frede's own frog, traced to vector from his drawing and set on a coin of Frog green, so his black line does the drawing, like a stamp.

  • Use the supplied files as they are: frog-logo.svg (vector, any size) or frog-logo-1024.png.
  • Never redraw, recolour, stretch or animate the frog.
  • Always the whole coin. The circle keeps one shape everywhere: a browser tab, the shop, an Instagram avatar.

Rules

Regras
  • Fan art is never sold. Prints and merch carry original work only; fan art can be shown, never sold.
  • Lines, not boxes: 1px hairlines, no shadows. Corners are near-square (2px); only small tags are round.
  • Give the art air. Dark ground, one green, generous space, nothing competing with the drawing.
